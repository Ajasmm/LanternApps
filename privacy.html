Privacy Policy

App: Masjid Alarm (app.masjidalarm) · Developer: AJAS M M · Last updated: 3 October 2026

In short

Masjid Alarm does not collect, share or sell any personal data. The app has no internet permission, no ads, no analytics and no accounts. Everything stays on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your prayer settings (Bank times, gaps, reminders, alarms), your answers ("On my way" / "Skip") and your language choice.
  • The location you enter or read once from your phone, as two numbers (latitude, longitude). It is used only to calculate prayer times on your phone.

All of this is stored only on your phone. It is not backed up to any server and is not sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes it. "Times here" uses your location for that screen only and does not save it.

Permissions and why

  • Notifications: to show Bank, Iqamah reminder and alarm notifications.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms): so alerts ring at the exact minute.
  • Full-screen alarm: to show the alarm over the lock screen.
  • Run at startup: to set your alarms again after the phone restarts.
  • Vibrate: for silent-phone alarms.
  • Location (only if you tap "Use my location"): read once to calculate prayer times. Approximate location is enough. Never tracked, never sent.
  • Do Not Disturb access (only if you turn on "Silence my phone"): to silence the phone during the jamaah and switch it back afterwards.

Sharing your setup

"Share my setup" creates a text code that you choose to send (for example on WhatsApp). The app itself sends nothing.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
