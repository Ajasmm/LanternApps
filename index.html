Masjid Alarm

Never miss Iqamath at your mosque

Prayer apps calculate one time for a whole city. Your masjid has its own Bank (Azan) and Iqamah times. Masjid Alarm follows your mosque:

  1. Type today's Bank time at your mosque once. It stays right all year.
  2. Before Iqamah you get a reminder: On my way or Skip.
  3. No answer? An alarm rings, so you never miss the jamaah.

Jumu'ah with Khutbah countdown · Subh wake-up alarm · silent phone during jamaah · works offline · no ads · English and Malayalam.

Get it on Google Play

Help

Alarms not ringing? Open the app → Setup → Alarm checklist, fix anything marked ✗, then tap "Test alarm in 1 minute". On Xiaomi, Vivo, Oppo, Realme and Samsung phones, also allow autostart and set battery to "No restrictions".
